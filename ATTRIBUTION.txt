This is adapted data. Each .egl file holds one 1-degree square of the Copernicus DEM GLO-90 (the public
copernicus-dem-90m bucket), with heights rounded to whole metres (the source's no-data value, where present, stored as
0) and losslessly compressed in Earth's DemTiles format (version 1). Nothing else is changed: the grid, its columns per
latitude band and its coverage are the source's.
