This is adapted data. Each .egl file holds one 1-degree square of the Copernicus DEM GLO-90 (the public
copernicus-dem-90m bucket), with heights rounded to whole metres (the source's no-data value, where present, stored as
0) and losslessly compressed in Earth's DemTiles format (version 1). Nothing else is changed: the grid, its columns per
latitude band and its coverage are the source's.

ESA, DLR or Airbus.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Water files (lakes-glo90-*.bin, rivers-outline-glo90-*.bin, rivers-flow-glo90-*.bin, dams-glo90-*.bin, streams-glo90-1.bin)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Adapted from Copernicus GLO-90 (its water body mask and heights; the notice above applies) and:

- HydroRIVERS v1.0 (river courses and discharges; for streams-glo90-1.bin, the reaches from 1 to 20 m3/s). Lehner, B.,
  Grill G. (2013): Global river hydrography and network routing: baseline data and new approaches to study the world's
  large river systems. Hydrological Processes, 27(15): 2171-2186. Data is available at www.hydrosheds.org. Used under
  the HydroSHEDS license (free for scientific, educational and commercial use, with attribution).
- HydroLAKES v1.0 (lake levels and the reservoirs left out). Messager, M.L., Lehner, B., Grill, G., Nedeva, I.,
  Schmitt, O. (2016): Estimating the volume and age of water stored in global lakes using a geo-statistical approach.
  Nature Communications, 7: 13603. Data is available at www.hydrosheds.org. Used under the HydroSHEDS license.
- Global Dam Watch v1.0 (the reservoirs HydroLAKES lacks, for dams-glo90-3.bin and -4.bin, lakes-glo90-2.bin to -5.bin
  and rivers-outline-glo90-4.bin and -5.bin). Lehner, B., Beames, P., Mulligan, M., Zarfl, C., De Felice, L., van
  Soesbergen, A., Thieme, M., Garcia de Leaniz, C., Anand, M., Belletti, B., et al. (2024): The Global Dam Watch database
  of river barrier and reservoir information for large-scale applications. Scientific Data 11, 1069.
  https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-024-03752-9. Licensed under CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
- GIRES v1.0, Global prevalence of non-perennial rivers and streams (for streams-glo90-1.bin: the streams without flow
  30 days a year or more where the coldest month is above -10 C are left out). Messager, M.L., Lehner, B., Cockburn, C.,
  Lamouroux, N., Pella, H., Snelder, T., Tockner, K., Trautmann, T., Watt, C., Datry, T. (2021): Global prevalence of
  non-perennial rivers and streams. Nature 594, 391-397. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-021-03565-5. Data: Messager,
  M.L., Lehner, B. (2021), figshare, https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.14633022. Licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0
  (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). streams-glo90-1.bin is distributed under the same licence,
  CC BY-SA 4.0.

How they were made: Earth's tools (bake_lake_outlines.py, bake_river_outlines.py, bake_river_flow.py,
bake_dam_removal.py, streams/bake_stream_tiles.py); the files ending -5, rivers-flow-glo90-2.bin and dams-glo90-4.bin
(Earth 2.2.0) put the water on GLO-90's own grid, whose heights are points, not cell centres (Earth decision 0092).
